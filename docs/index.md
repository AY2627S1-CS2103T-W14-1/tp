**CampusContacts is a desktop application for university students and staff to manage contacts within their university community.**

* If you are interested in using CampusContacts, head over to the [_Quick Start_ section of the **User Guide**](UserGuide.html#quick-start).
* If you are interested in developing CampusContacts, the [**Developer Guide**](DeveloperGuide.html) is a good place to start.
